Notes:
- The WebGPU path is used for single-point energy/force jobs only. MD and FIRE
  relaxation need a synchronous kernel per step and keep the WASM/CPU path.
- Every WebGPU energy job is cross-checked against the TypeScript f64 kernel
  before the result is presented; the cross-check is shown in the UI and a
  failure is visible.
- An about:blank probe does not expose navigator.gpu; the app runs on
  http://localhost (a secure context), where the adapter is available.
- On a browser without an adapter, the worker falls back to WASM and records
  the reason; forcing backend "webgpu" refuses instead of silently using CPU.
